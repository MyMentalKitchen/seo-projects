# Fixes #5, #6, #7: On-Page Title, Meta & Email Fixes

Apply these changes in Wix Editor > each page > SEO Settings.

---

## Fix #5: Our Food Page Title

**Current:** `OUR FOOD | GipsyHill_Smokehouse`
**Problem:** Generic, uppercase, underscore in brand name -- tells Google nothing about the food.

**Change to:** `Hog Roast, BBQ & Spit Roast Menus | The Gipsy Hill Smokehouse`

Steps:
1. Wix Editor > navigate to the "Our Food" page
2. Click page menu (three dots) > SEO Basics
3. Update the **SEO Title** field
4. Save and Publish

---

## Fix #6: Meta Keywords Cleanup

Meta keywords are ignored by Google, but sloppy ones signal neglect to
anyone inspecting your source. Fix or remove them.

### Wedding Page (`/hog-roast-wedding-caterer-london`)
**Current:** `about, the, gipsy, hill, smokhouse` (typo: "smokhouse")
**Change to:** `hog roast wedding caterer london, wedding catering london, spit roast wedding`

### Our Food Page (`/our-food`)
**Current:** `our, food`
**Change to:** `hog roast menu, bbq catering menu, spit roast menu london`

### Homepage
**Current:** `hog roast, hog roast caterer`
**Change to:** `hog roast caterer london, bbq catering london, wedding hog roast, spit roast catering`

Steps per page:
1. Wix Editor > navigate to the page
2. Click page menu > SEO Basics > Advanced SEO
3. Find the **meta keywords** tag and update the value
4. Save and Publish

## Fix #7: Standardise Email Addresses

**Problem:** Two different email domains are used across the site:
- `timclements@thegipsyhillsmokehouse.com` (with "the")
- `timclements@gipsyhillsmokehouse.com` (without "the")

This confuses customers and weakens NAP (Name, Address, Phone) consistency
for local SEO signals.

**Action:** Pick ONE canonical email and use it everywhere.

Recommended: `timclements@thegipsyhillsmokehouse.com` (matches the domain name).

### Pages to update:
| Page | Current Email | Action |
|------|--------------|--------|
| Homepage | `timclements@thegipsyhillsmokehouse.com` | Keep |
| Wedding page | Both addresses appear | Change `gipsyhillsmokehouse.com` to `thegipsyhillsmokehouse.com` |
| Contact page | `timclements@thegipsyhillsmokehouse.com` | Keep |

Also update the email in:
- Any Wix contact forms (Wix Editor > Form Settings)
- Google Business Profile (if one exists)
- Facebook page info
- Any third-party directories or citations
