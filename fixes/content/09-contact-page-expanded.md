### What to Include in Your Enquiry

To help us give you an accurate quote, please let us know:

- **Date of your event** (or approximate timeframe)
- **Number of guests** expected
- **Type of event** (wedding, party, corporate, festival, etc.)
- **Venue location** and any access details
- **Menu preferences** (hog roast, lamb, BBQ, mixed meats)
- **Any dietary requirements** (vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, allergies)
- **Budget range** (helps us recommend the right package)
