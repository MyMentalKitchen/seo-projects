## Frequently Asked Questions About Wedding Hog Roasts

### How many guests can a hog roast feed?

A whole hog roast typically feeds 80 to 120 guests, depending on the size of the pig and the number of side dishes served. For smaller weddings of 30 to 60 guests, we offer half-hog options or our porchetta menu. For larger celebrations of 150 guests or more, we can provide multiple hogs or combine a hog roast with spit-roasted lamb and BBQ options.

### How much does wedding hog roast catering cost?

Pricing depends on your guest count, menu selections, and level of service. As a guide, our wedding packages typically range from around 15 to 35 per head, which includes the hog roast, sides, bread rolls, sauces, and serving staff. Canapes, desserts, bar services, and evening snacks are available as additions. Contact us for a personalised quote tailored to your wedding.

### How far in advance should we book?

We recommend booking at least 3 to 6 months before your wedding date, especially for summer weddings (May to September) which are our busiest period. However, we can sometimes accommodate shorter notice bookings depending on availability. The sooner you get in touch, the more flexibility we have with your menu and timing.

### Do you cater for vegetarians and guests with dietary requirements?

Absolutely. Every wedding is different, and we always ensure vegetarian guests are well looked after with options like grilled halloumi, roasted vegetable platters, and stuffed peppers. Vegan and gluten-free options are also available with advance notice. We ask for full dietary information during the menu planning stage so nobody is left out.

### What happens if it rains on our wedding day?

We're experienced outdoor caterers and have cooked in all weather conditions across the UK. Our hog roast equipment is designed to operate rain or shine. For the serving area, we recommend your venue provides a covered space or marquee, which most outdoor wedding venues already have. We bring our own gazebo for the cooking station as standard.

### Do you provide staff and equipment?

Yes. Our packages include the hog roast machine, all cooking equipment, serving tables, disposable or eco-friendly plates and cutlery, napkins, and trained serving staff. We arrive several hours before service to set up and begin the slow roast, and we handle all cleanup afterwards. You don't need to lift a finger.

### What area do you cover for weddings?

We cover a 50-mile radius around London as standard, including Greater London, Kent, Surrey, Sussex, Essex, and Hertfordshire. We've also catered weddings further afield, including in Cornwall, Scotland, and even Portugal. Travel charges may apply for venues outside our standard area.

### Can we taste the food before booking?

We occasionally hold tasting events and are sometimes present at food markets and festivals where you can sample our cooking. Get in touch and we'll let you know about upcoming opportunities. We also have a portfolio of photos and videos from previous weddings to give you a clear idea of what to expect.
