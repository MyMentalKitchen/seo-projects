## Barbecue Menus

### Free-Range Marinated Porchetta

Our signature dish. We source whole free-range pigs from trusted British farms and marinate them porchetta-style with fennel seeds, rosemary, garlic, sea salt, and black pepper. The result is succulent, herb-infused meat with irresistibly crispy crackling.

You may have known us from Borough Market or Broadway Market, where we built our reputation for London's finest porchetta. We now operate as private caterers, bringing that same market-quality food to weddings, private parties, and corporate events.

We also love catering rugby festivals, food fairs, and one-day events across London and the South East.

### BBQ Chicken

Our corn-fed, free-range chickens are marinated with fresh lemon juice and our house-made piri piri sauce, a nod to our Portuguese heritage. Slow-cooked over charcoal, the chickens are succulent, smoky, and bursting with flavour.

Available as whole birds, halves, or pulled chicken for rolls and wraps. A crowd-pleaser at every event we cater.

### BBQ Roast Pork

Free-range loins of pork, hand-rubbed with a blend of fennel, rosemary, and garlic, then slow-roasted until the fat renders down to a golden, crispy crust. Served sliced with our homemade apple sauce and fresh artisan rolls.

Perfect for smaller gatherings where a whole hog isn't needed, but you still want that authentic Gipsy Hill Smokehouse flavour.

### Spit Roast Lamb Cooked Over Wood

Our whole lambs are spit-roasted slowly over seasoned hardwood, producing meat that is tender, juicy, and deeply smoky. We use a traditional spit technique that allows the fat to baste the meat as it turns, locking in flavour.

Served with mint sauce, roasted vegetables, and warm flatbreads. An ideal centrepiece for spring and summer celebrations.

### BBQ Specials

Follow us on Instagram and Facebook to find out where we'll be next. Our rotating specials include:

- **Flaming Chourico** -- Portuguese smoked sausage, flambeed tableside
- **Sardine Festas** -- whole grilled sardines, a traditional Portuguese summer celebration
- **Suckling Pig** -- a smaller, more delicate roast with incredibly tender meat
- **Beef Brisket** -- low-and-slow smoked brisket with our house BBQ rub
- **Spicy Pork Ribs** -- dry-rubbed and glazed with a smoky chipotle sauce

### Sides & Accompaniments

Every hog roast and BBQ comes with a selection of freshly prepared sides:

- Classic coleslaw with a tangy dressing
- Mixed leaf salads with seasonal dressings
- Roasted Mediterranean vegetables
- Fluffy naan bread and artisan rolls
- Homemade apple sauce and stuffing
- A selection of house-made sauces and condiments

### Dietary Requirements

We take dietary needs seriously. Our menus can accommodate:

- **Vegetarian options** -- grilled halloumi, roasted vegetable platters, stuffed peppers
- **Vegan options** -- available on request with advance notice
- **Gluten-free** -- most of our meats are naturally gluten-free; GF bread rolls available
- **Allergen information** -- full allergen details provided for every dish on request

Talk to us about your specific requirements and we'll design a menu that works for everyone at your event.
